FR and ES promotions end 30/06/2026; Germany runs to 30.09.2026; and Spain's promo copy even contradicts itself on the page. The European offer needs one coordinated calendar and one consistent line.

France leads with it, Germany frames it as receive-only, Spain barely mentions it. Compliance is the one reason every SMB buys now — it should be the universal hero spine across all markets.

Playbook · the WiSE Active standard

What "good" looks like for a Sage Active country page

The standard the demos in this hub are built to, and the live sites are measured against — the same discipline as the France accountant playbook.

Do

  • Lead with the local regulatory clock, dated and specific (Sept 2026; VeriFactu; E-Rechnung; ATCUD/SAF-T).
  • Name the compliance mechanism — plateforme agréée, VeriFactu, ZUGFeRD/X-Rechnung, SVAT — as proof, not just "ready".
  • Show real, named customer voices — three minimum.
  • Localise every screenshot to the market's language and client names.
  • State the offer once, consistently, with a single end-date.
  • Make Copilot concrete with a real question-and-answer.

Don't

  • Say "compliant" without naming the mechanism or the date.
  • Ship the shared pan-European screenshots with foreign names and typos.
  • Run a carousel with a single quote — or none at all.
  • Contradict the promo between hero, cards and footnote.
  • Leave a silent scope gap (payroll in FR/DE, absent in ES) unexplained.
  • Use "migration" for transitions — use accompanied-transition language.
